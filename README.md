**Garuda Courier — Nusantara Ring Run.** Fly the bush plane "Cendrawasih-9" over the Indonesian archipelago at golden hour: turquoise sea, green volcanic islands, drifting clouds and a low sun. Three sorties: Ring Run (fly through 8 glowing rings), Mail Drop (land the mail on 3 island strips), and Storm Rescue (reach the climber's beacon before the fuel runs out, fighting a crosswind). Pure Canvas 2D chase view with a lit amber HUD — altitude, speed, heading with compass tape, fuel, damage, artificial horizon and turn coordinator — and WebAudio synth engine, wind and ring chimes. No network requests, no external libraries.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Controls: W/S throttle, A/D bank & turn, I/K trim, R restart, P pause, M mute. Press 1/2/3 on the title screen to pick a sortie, ENTER to fly.
